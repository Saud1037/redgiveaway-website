What it does

Three jobs, done well.

Giveaways

Start a timed giveaway with one command. Members join by reacting, and the bot picks winners at random when time is up.

Reroll

A winner didn't claim? Pick a new one for any ended giveaway without starting over.

Greetings

Welcome new members with your own message, in as many channels as you like, with optional auto-delete.

Set up in three steps

No dashboard, no account.

  1. Invite

    Add the bot to your server and keep its role above the channels it works in.

  2. Check permissions

    Give it Send Messages, Embed Links, Read Message History and Add Reactions.

  3. Run

    Type !gstart in any channel to launch your first giveaway.
