Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 16, 2026

This policy explains what data RedGiveaway ("the bot", "we") collects when you add it to a Discord server or use its commands, how that data is used, and the choices you have. By using the bot you agree to this policy.

Data we collect

We only store what the bot needs to run giveaways and greetings:

  • Server data: server (guild) IDs, channel IDs, role IDs, and the settings admins configure, such as greeting messages and giveaway channels.
  • Giveaway data: message IDs, prize, end time, number of winners, host ID, and the IDs of users who entered or won.
  • User IDs: Discord user IDs of members who enter giveaways, host giveaways, or join a server where greetings are enabled.
  • Error logs: technical error details used to find and fix bugs. These may include IDs of the server, channel, or command involved.

Message content

The bot reads messages only to detect commands and to process giveaway entries. It does not store the content of your messages, and it does not read direct messages between users.

How we use data

  • To start, run, end, and reroll giveaways.
  • To send greeting messages to new members.
  • To keep your server settings between restarts.
  • To diagnose errors and improve reliability.

We do not sell your data, use it for advertising, or share it with third parties, except the hosting and database providers needed to run the bot, or when the law requires it.

Data retention and deletion

Giveaway records are kept for as long as needed to run and reroll them. When the bot is removed from a server, that server's data can be deleted on request. You can ask us to delete data linked to your user ID or your server at any time through the support server.

Security

Data is stored with reputable hosting providers and access is limited to the bot's developer. No system is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

Children

Discord requires users to be at least 13 years old (or the minimum age in your country). The bot is not intended for anyone younger, and we do not knowingly collect data from them.

Third-party services

The bot runs on Discord, so your use of it is also governed by Discord's Privacy Policy and Terms of Service.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The date at the top shows the latest revision, and continued use of the bot means you accept the changes.

Contact

Questions or deletion requests? Join the support server and open a ticket.
